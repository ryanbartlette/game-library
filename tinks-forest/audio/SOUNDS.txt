Tink's Forest - sound list
==========================
Every clip here is trimmed, loop-joined where needed, and volume-matched for the game.
Footstep, claw, rustle and creak files are "sprites": many short sounds placed one after another
at a fixed spacing, so the game can pick a different one each time.

music/
  Ryan's original score (made in Suno, Nov 2026). "Intro, then loops" files hold the whole song up to the loop's end,
  that end already blended into the loop's start, plus a second more; the game plays the intro once, then repeats the loop.

  02-a-quiet-breath.mp3             pause menu. Intro, then loops 37.13-112.36 s
  03-asleep-on-the-sofa.mp3         the cozy house at night, and by the home tree. Intro, then loops 25.19-145.26 s
  04-the-tunnel-of-old-trees.mp3    the walk to and through the tunnel. Intro, then loops 35.43-154.39 s
  05-into-the-magical-forest.mp3    the forest reveal as you leave the tunnel (plays once)
  06-petals-on-the-path.mp3         spring days, now and then (plays once each time)
  07-the-long-green-afternoon.mp3   summer days, now and then
  08-the-harvest-waltz.mp3          autumn days, now and then
  09-snow-on-the-lanterns.mp3       winter days, now and then
  10-the-furnace-keeper.mp3         farmhouse boiler room (loop only)
  11-waiting-in-the-rain.mp3        near the bus stop during the rain chapter. Intro, then loops 13.49-105.37 s
  12-a-seed-that-dreams-of-sky.mp3  the magic beanstalk growing and the climb (loop only)
  13-the-black-cat-waits.mp3        Jack's theme: meeting him at the tunnel's end, and whenever his card shows. Intro, then loops 19.78-117.54 s
  14-lantern-light.mp3              the forest at night (after the opening). Intro, then loops 30.63-118.07 s
  15-the-glint-in-the-stream.mp3    the boat chapter, until the boat is found. Intro, then loops 38.8-102.79 s
  16-where-the-sky-begins.mp3       resting at the top of the beanstalk. Intro, then loops 31.93-100.45 s
  17-leap-of-faith.mp3              starts the instant she leaps from the top, plays once
  18-home-to-the-great-tree.mp3     the farewell at the Great Tree (plays once)
  19-spring-comes-again.mp3         after the farewell, once, as free roam begins
  20a-gift-found.mp3                a short chime each time a new gift joins the row

  day-forest.mp3 / night-forest.mp3 general forest by day / night
  dawn-chorus.mp3                   the first minute or two after night turns to morning

  evening-cicadas.mp3               golden hour (spring-autumn), faintly on summer nights
  night-crickets.mp3                warm nights (summer loudest, none in winter)
  wind.mp3                          wind in spring and summer
  wind-autumn.mp3 / wind-winter.mp3 wind in autumn / winter (winter always faintly there)
  rain.mp3                          heavy rain, swelling and easing

  stream-babble.mp3                 louder the closer you are to the stream

cats/  (Tink, Luca and Jack - Jack uses Luca's voice)
  luca-meow-1..3, meow-high, tink-meow-1..4, trill, purr-1, purr-2, purr-meow,
  yowl-1..3, hiss, scared, scream

animals/
  birdsong-1/2, robin-chirp-1..3, crow-caw, wing-flap, cuckoo (spring days), owl-hoot (nights),
  woodpecker-drumming, squirrel-chirp / chatter / tease, frog-croak, dragonfly-buzz (close to a dragonfly)

actions/
  footsteps-leaves (forest floor), footsteps-dry-leaves (autumn), footsteps-snow (winter), footsteps-wood (tower room, veranda, logs),
  footsteps-stone (rocks, bridges, stepping stones), footsteps-water, grass-rustle (moving through grass),
  landing-thump, claws-on-bark (climbing), paw-swat, creaky-floor (tower room),
  water-splash, water-jump

magic/
  item-found-chime (gifts, Jack, last puff caught), jack-shimmer (Jack appears / fades),
  sparkle (a gift appears, the beanstalk becomes climbable), beanstalk-growing,
  puff-giggle (a moss puff runs off), puff-pop (catching a puff), wind-chime (farmhouse chime in the wind)

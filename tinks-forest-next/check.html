Visual check: live vs staging

Loads the live game twice and staging once, takes the same 40 camera shots each time, and compares them pixel by pixel on your graphics card. The two live runs show what moves naturally; only differences beyond that are counted. The clock is held still during the shots so the light can't shift between runs. Takes about twelve minutes; keep this tab in front. It never touches your save: time of day, season and weather are put back after each run.
